mention links. Researcher plans; Reviewer independently checks plan/data; Implementer
runs smoke; Reviewer checks smoke; Implementer runs the approved budget; Reviewer checks

evidence and verdicts in the experiment note and issue. Do not self-approve or interpret
completed training as scientific success. Framework choice is driven by the question.
