distinguish reproduced facts from hypotheses. Understand the architecture and evaluation
protocol before adapting it. Specify tuned baselines, metrics, seed list, uncertainty,
data constraints, compute budget and stopping rule. Prefer RecBole when it fits; choose
PyTorch or Lightning when required by the method. Hand a versioned plan to Reviewer.
