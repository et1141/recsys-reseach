---
name: experiment-notes
description: Maintain minimal atomic experiment notes and thesis evidence links for this research workspace.
---

Use one notes/EXP-NNN-short-name.md file per scientific question, following
notes/templates/experiment.md. Multiple seeds and reruns belong in the same note;
a materially different hypothesis gets a new ID. Do not create a note for every log.

Keep the note roughly one screen (aim for 250 words excluding a small results table).
Preserve user writing. Record question, plan, evidence links, result, review and decision.
Update only what changed. Unknown means pending, never invent an outcome or citation.
Use [[wiki links]] between notes. Link code/configs/artifacts with normal Markdown paths.
Keep detailed logs, complete configurations and metrics in artifacts and W&B.

Record reviewer identity, stage, verdict and evidence. Only the human marks accepted.
When a finding is accepted, add a link under its thesis claim in notes/Home.md with
one sentence describing the supported conclusion and its limits. Do not write thesis
chapters automatically. Leave one visible next action and keep the idea inbox in Home.
